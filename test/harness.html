Ham radio repeaters extend the range of handheld and mobile radios across the Houston metro area. Each repeater listing in this guide includes the output frequency, the offset, and the access tone needed to key the repeater. Most repeaters in the Houston area operate on the two meter band or the seventy centimeter band.

Repeater coverage depends on antenna height, transmit power, and terrain. The repeater listings below are grouped by band and sorted by frequency so you can quickly program your radio. Always identify with your callsign when using any repeater.
